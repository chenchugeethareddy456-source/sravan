Our approach

Maison Elowen began with a simple frustration: too many clothes, not enough of them worth keeping. Each piece is chosen or made in small batches, in linen, wool and cotton, in a palette that moves easily from one season to the next. Nothing is designed to be seasonal. Everything is designed to last.
